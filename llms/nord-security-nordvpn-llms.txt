> NordVPN
A leading Virtual Private Network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012 and headquartered in Panama. NordVPN offers:
Encrypted Internet Tunnels
AES-256-GCM encryption, a strict no-logs policy and an automatic kill switch to ensure user data and online activities remain private and secure.
Global Server Network
Thousands of servers across 127 countries, optimized for speed, secure browsing, streaming, P2P file sharing.
Advanced Privacy Features
Obfuscated servers to defeat VPN blocking, Double VPN for multi-hop encryption, Onion over VPN integration, and DNS/IPv6 leak protection.
Cross-Platform Support
Native apps for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and browser extensions, plus manual setup on routers and other devices.
Use cases include protecting sensitive data on public Wi-Fi, maintaining privacy online, accessing home content while away, and evading censorship.
